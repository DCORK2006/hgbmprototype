OUR PURPOSE

Stories of triumph. Stories that move people forward.

Higher Ground Books & Media is an independent publisher located in Springfield, Ohio. We publish fiction, children's books, nonfiction, personal stories, and resources for ministry and personal exploration of the Christian faith.

Our purpose is to empower, inspire, and educate through the sharing of personal experiences.
